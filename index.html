    Release Playbook

    What a hot or cool print usually does
    1 · It's the surprise that counts

    Markets already price in the consensus forecast. The move comes from how far the actual number lands from it, and from revisions to prior months.

    2 · Know the market's worry

    When inflation is the fear, strong data is bad for stocks (higher yields). When growth is the fear, strong data is a relief. Watch how stocks and the 10-yr react to the first big print each month.

    3 · The 2-year leads

    The 2-yr yield reacts most to changes in Fed expectations. A big move in it after a release tells you the market changed its rate path.

    4 · First move ≠ final move

    Initial reactions at 7:30 CT often reverse by the open. Wait for the cash session before drawing conclusions for clients.
